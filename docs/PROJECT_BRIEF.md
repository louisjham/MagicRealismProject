# Project Brief — Magical Realism Invoker

## Elevator Pitch

A controller-free mixed-reality experience that transforms any living room into
a low-poly fantasy world through deliberate hand rituals, then populates it with
autonomous creatures whose behaviour emerges unpredictably from the player's choices.

## Core Experience Loop

1. **Perceive** — Player enters the experience. Their real room is fully visible, unmodified.
2. **Awaken the floor** — Both hands palm-down for 5 seconds. Flash-paper transition reveals fantasy stone beneath the carpet.
3. **Shape the walls** — Point at each wall to transmute it. Cave or stone; player chooses the character.
4. **Light the space** — Sideways fist summons a magical torch. Throw it, place it, colour the shadows.
5. **Open windows** — Palm-out on a wall tears a heavy, vaporous portal into adjacent reality.
6. **Seed the world** — Draw glyphs in the air. Strange shapes materialize moments later.
7. **Watch it breathe** — Creatures wake, react, pursue, flee, conflict. The story writes itself.

## What Makes It Different

- **No preset experience.** Every session is unique due to seeded randomness + player choices.
- **No menus.** Every interaction is a hand gesture.
- **The room is the canvas.** Real physical space anchors the fantasy.
- **Organic emergence.** Creatures follow autonomous rules — the player sets conditions, not outcomes.
