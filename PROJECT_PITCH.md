Project Pitch: Magical Realism Invoker

Magical Realism Invoker is a controller-free, mixed-reality spatial experience that transforms any mundane living room into an immersive fantasy workshop through intuitive hand-tracking.

Rather than relying on traditional UI menus or handheld controllers, players use ritualistic gestures to rewrite their physical environment: swiping surfaces to transmute drywall and carpet into low-poly stone masonry, tearing open dimensional portals into alternative spaces, and conjuring dynamic light sources. By bridging physical room anchors with procedural state-driven lighting—where placed artifacts alter environmental parameters—Magical Realism Invoker demonstrates how spatial computing can turn everyday architecture into an interactive, reactive canvas.
